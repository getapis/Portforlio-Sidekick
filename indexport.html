About me

The person behind
the work

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Marine Engineering — but life had bigger plans for me. I chose people over machines, and what followed was six years of building something I'm genuinely proud of: a career in Customer Experience and Quality Assurance that took me from front-line support to leading a team of 15 analysts.

I've worked across telecom, e-commerce, fintech, and technical support — always in high-pressure, high-volume environments where mistakes cost real money and real relationships. That background makes me a different kind of VA.

I've navigated some of life's hardest moments — watching a parent battle cancer, losing my lola, overcoming my own personal battles. Every one of those experiences shaped how I show up for the people I work for. This work is personal to me.

Now I bring everything I've built to Australian gym and studio owners — as their Super Sidekick VA. The person who protects their time, stabilises their operations, and makes sure nothing slips through the cracks.
