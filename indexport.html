About
Traded a marine engineering degree for a headset — best call I made.

I hold a BS in Marine Engineering, but six years ago I chose people over machines. What followed was six years building a career in Customer Experience and Quality Assurance — starting on the frontline and eventually leading a team of 15 QA analysts.

That team worked across telecom, e-commerce, fintech, and technical support — high-volume, high-pressure environments where a missed follow-up costs real money and real trust. That's the standard I bring to a gym's inbox.

I've also worked through real setbacks outside of work — the kind that teach you patience and follow-through you don't get from a training manual. It's part of why I show up steady, even when the week gets messy.

Right now I'm placed as a General VA with a gym owner through WrkPod, and putting everything I learn on the floor into Super Sidekick — a VA practice built specifically for gym and studio owners.
